## Comment text (draft)

> We can reproduce this under concurrency on a self-hosted build (fork at `c2b7ac44`; the files below are unchanged at
> `main` `91eefbf9`). A private link with `maxUsageCount` 1 and 10 parallel bookings through it on distinct free slots:
> **all 10 are ACCEPTED** (10 × 200) and `usageCount` ends at 1, in 50 of 50 trials in each of four CI runs. With
> `maxUsageCount` 3: all 10 ACCEPTED, `usageCount` 3, also 50 of 50 per run. No request gets a 4xx.
>
> This may be why a sequential retest does not show it: the link is checked when the booking page loads, and usage is
> counted only after the booking exists. `BookingEventHandlerService.onBookingCreatedOrRescheduled` runs the private-link
> usage update inside `Promise.allSettled` (`packages/features/bookings/lib/onBookingEvents/BookingEventHandlerService.ts`
> L44–67), and a failed `validateAndIncrementUsage` is only logged. Nothing stops a booking once the counter is full.
> Validating and incrementing in one atomic step before the booking is created (as #29839 proposed) would close both the
> sequential and the concurrent case.
>
> Reproduction: https://github.com/Jayami123/k6-load-concurrency-suite, specs
> `tests/cal/races/cal-race-006-private-link.spec.ts` and `tests/cal/races/cal-race-008-private-link-cap.spec.ts`. CI
> evidence (per-trial JSON): https://github.com/Jayami123/k6-load-concurrency-suite/releases/tag/p6-phase4 and
> https://github.com/Jayami123/k6-load-concurrency-suite/releases/tag/p6-phase3, file
> `race-histograms-cal-state/P6-CAL-RACE-006.json` / `-008.json` in each evidence zip.
